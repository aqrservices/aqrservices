Personal Introduction

Diagnose. Structure. Execute.

With a practice spanning business strategy, data analytics, pitch development, content systems, and brand execution, Arsalan approaches every engagement the way a consultant does: diagnose the problem, structure the solution, and execute with precision.

His work bridges the gap between high-level strategic thinking and ground-level delivery — making professional-grade consulting accessible to founders who need it most.

Beyond AQR, he co-founded Trend Mosaic Media — a youth-driven freelance collective on a mission to create real economic opportunity for young talent, proving that execution and social impact aren't mutually exclusive.

Currently pursuing his BBA at The Islamia College of Science and Commerce, he is building toward a career at the forefront of international management consulting — with AQR Services and Trend Mosaic Media as the twin proving grounds where strategy meets execution.

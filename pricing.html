Policies & Terms

Pricing Policies

Clear, upfront terms — no hidden fees, no post-delivery surprises.

⚡
Rush Delivery Fee

+30–50% on base price for turnarounds under 48 hours. Accepted subject to current capacity — always confirm availability first.

🔄
Revision Policy

Starter: 1 round · Growth: 2 rounds · Partner: Unlimited within agreed scope. Scope changes are quoted separately.

🎯
Custom Projects

Quoted after a free discovery call — no standard package required. Complex multi-vertical engagements are always scoped individually.

📊
Margin Principle

All projects priced to maintain a 40–60% agency margin after execution costs — ensuring sustainable quality on every engagement.

💳
Payment Terms

Starter: Full upfront · Growth & Partner: 50% advance, 50% on delivery. International payments via Wise or PayPal (USD). GST applicable per Indian norms.

🔒
Confidentiality

All client information and briefs treated with complete confidentiality. NDA available on request for sensitive engagements.
